Hi 👋, I'm Antonio, and I'm currently a DPhil student @ [FLAIR](https://foersterlab.com) & [TVG](https://torrvision.com/index.html), University of Oxford.

My research focuses on understanding and quantifying what LLMs _think_ they know, and how they represent uncertainty and belief.

Beyond my PhD, I am very interested on the intersection of mathematics and AI: both how AI can be used to advance mathematical research, and how mathematical inductive biases can help make deep learning architectures more efficient and effective. 

Outside of AI, I am also working on developing solvers which can efficiently compute steady-state solutions to PDEs.
